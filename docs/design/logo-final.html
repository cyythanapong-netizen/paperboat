4 · W2 ripple (wave 25% higher, boat bobs a little less)

The water dots lift in sequence (a crest travels left → right), and the boat bobs as the crest passes under it. Wave travel 3.75px (was 3.0); boat bob 1.0px (was 1.5). Idle cycle every ~18s. Values scale with the mark so the bar stays subtle.

transform only · no layout shift · collapses to 0ms under prefers-reduced-motion

Idle: ripple every ~18s, ripples for ~7% of the cycle, then rests (the “once in a while” behaviour).

6 · Implementation notes

  • Logo.tsx. Add an S2 component using the original 62×42 viewBox: two-tone sail triangles (M31 0 L31 16 L16 16 Z and M31 0 L46 16 L31 16 Z), trapezoid hull (M0 18 L62 18 L48 32 L14 32 Z), and the 5 pixel water dots (rects at x 0/14/28/42/56, y=36, 6×6, crispEdges). Merged variant: hull top/bottom shift up 2 (M0 16 L62 16 L48 30 L14 30 Z). Prop variant="s2" or auto by height.
  • Thresholds. height >= 32 → pixel mark. 20 <= height < 32 → S2 with gap. height < 20 → S2 merged.
  • Bar. <Logo height={24} /> becomes S2 (gap variant). Water no longer overlaps the hull.
  • Favicon. Stays pixel art in the brand palette (64px app icon).
  • Ripple (W2). CSS keyframe on the water group: animation: ripple 1.4s var(--ease) 1 with translateY(calc(var(--r)*-1)) at 30% and calc(var(--r)*0.33) at 60%. Boat bob on the whole mark: translateY(calc(var(--b)*-1)) at 30%. Idle: 18s cycle, ripple for ~7% then rest. Run-linked: toggle a class on the wordmark on run.started / run.finished. Scale --r/--b per instance (stage 3.75/1.0, bar 1.9/0.5).
  • Reduced motion. @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { animation: none; } — mark never moves.
  • Motion budget. Transform only, no layout shift.
